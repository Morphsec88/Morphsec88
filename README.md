# Welcome, Builders, Cloners, and Partners

Thank you for stopping by. This is not a typical polished tech resume or a collection of corporate buzzwords. It is an honest look at who I am, how I got here, and why I build what I build.

---

### From Physical Machines to Digital Systems
I grew up quiet and reserved. While other kids played with toys, I put mine away at age 10 to work with real materials—drilling, carving, and shaping things by hand. By 12, I bought my own angle grinder and drill. Growing up in a single-parent household raised by my mother, we moved often, and I found peace in solitude and creation.

I attended trade school, mastered a wide range of crafts—largely self-taught—and spent 15 years working in Budapest. Alongside my day jobs, I spent my free time in my workshop designing and building custom machinery and practical mechanical solutions.

### A Shift to the Countryside and Caregiving
Seeking peace and quiet, I eventually left Budapest for the countryside to focus on real estate development. My goal was simple: work on long-term property projects while staying close to care for my chronically ill mother. 

Life had other plans. The noise and chaos of renovation proved too overwhelming for her, so we rented a quiet place for her in a nearby town. A year later, she suffered a serious fall that left her mobility unstable. Over the past three years, her health has declined rapidly, showing clear signs of dementia alongside other severe conditions. Gradually, I had to step away from my physical projects to become her full-time caregiver. Today, all construction is completely on hold.

### Discovering Code and AI (May 2026)
Full-time caregiving is exhausting. For a year and a half, I made lighthearted and educational videos on TikTok just to keep my mind from breaking under the pressure. Eventually, I realized I needed to channel my energy into something with lasting impact.

On **May 14–15, 2026**, while checking on an old project, I received an email mentioning that artificial intelligence could now assist in writing code. On **May 16**, I registered on GitHub and built my very first repositories.

Driven by the same engineering mindset I used in my workshop, I began deeply researching the tech sector and asking hard questions about its unresolved bottlenecks. AI became my sounding board, and my brain refused to shut off—often keeping me awake until dawn as I conceptualized new data transmission architectures and system solutions.

### Where Things Stand Today
Today, I have multiple active projects. Some are open-source and public on this profile, while others are proprietary and kept confidential due to their commercial nature. 

I am at a difficult crossroads:
* **No Industry Network:** Coming from a hands-on trades and engineering background rather than traditional academia or Big Tech, I have no established industry connections.
* **Caregiving Reality:** My mother’s condition requires constant attention, leaving very little time or energy to push these projects forward alone.
* **Untapped Potential:** Progress is currently slow, not because the ideas lack value, but because I am fighting an uphill battle without resources.

### Why I Am Sharing This
I am publishing this story to be completely transparent about my situation. To continue developing these systems—and to properly manage my caregiving responsibilities—I need financial backing, sponsorship, or serious partnerships. With the right support, I am ready to dedicate myself fully to bringing these projects to life.

Please understand that sharing my personal struggles does not mean my work is undervalued. **Groundbreaking solutions are not for sale for pocket change.** I know the worth of the architectures I am building, and I am looking for partners who recognize real engineering potential when they see it.

Thank you for your time, your attention, and your interest in my work. I wish you productive building—and I hope, together, we can create a better world.
